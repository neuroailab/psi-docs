About this map & the profile sources +

The room is a hand-built digital interpretation of the lab’s whiteboard seating sketch and reference photos. Furniture, scale, and wall placement are approximate. Desk labels indicate assigned places in the sketch—not live presence or availability.

Each completed biography links to its public source. Portraits are reused from Klemen’s collaborator directory or the person’s public profile; credits are available in each card. Unverified profiles use initials. The two unlabeled desks stay unlabeled; RENE is the YAM robot station, not a person. Research filters are broad groupings, not an exhaustive account of anyone’s work.

Want to correct a seat or add a portrait? Let Klemen know. Profile sources checked September 21, 2026.
