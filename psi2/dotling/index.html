Dotling MTP tuning windows

AuriStream predicts 40 future speech tokens (≈200 ms ≈ one word) and its embeddings become word-decodable. Is that a coincidence, or does the optimal multi-token-prediction horizon n* track the temporal grain of the data? Eight synthetic dot languages, every structural knob controlled, ~200 GPT+MTP models — results stream in below as the 8×A40 sweep runs.

Probe accuracy vs MTP horizon n

Each panel: linear-probe accuracy on frozen embeddings vs the training horizon n (log axis), seeds averaged. Dotted line = predicted n*. Glint adds a fast “shimmer” channel probe (should prefer n=1); cascade adds a phrase probe (should prefer n≈12).

The loss-vs-quality dissociation

P5 — head-1 loss worsens, generations improve

Training with more heads makes the next-token loss worse — yet head-1-only generations parse better against the true lexicon and score lower oracle perplexity under the true language model.

Model-free structure

Mutual information by lag

Plug-in MI (Miller–Madow corrected) between tokens k apart, computed from 1M raw tokens per language before any model is trained. The knee is the x-axis of the money plot.

Phases 2–3 — substitution and ablations
